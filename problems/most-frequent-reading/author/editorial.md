# Private organizer notes: Most Frequent Reading

Count all readings in a map, then choose by descending count and ascending numeric value. Online updates of the best pair are also valid. Sorting is an alternative correct approach.

Time: {'Frequency counting': 'Expected O(N) with a hash table'}. Auxiliary space: O(D).

Alternative correct algorithms are allowed. Expected hash-map time is not a worst-case guarantee. Account for materialized input, output, and stacks separately. Tests assess observable behavior.
